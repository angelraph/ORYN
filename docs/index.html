Read the docs
Does ORYN ever hold my money?

No. Your funds sit in your own vault contract, and only your wallet can withdraw or change rules. The agent can only trigger the split you already approved.

What does it cost?

ORYN takes no cut of your payments. You pay normal Celo gas, fractions of a cent, for the transactions you sign. The agent pays its own gas and fees for every split.

How fast is a payment split?

The agent checks every 15 seconds, so a split usually lands within about 30 seconds of a payment confirming.

What if I get paid before setting rules?

The money is safe in your vault as savings. Withdraw it any time, and set your rules so the next payment is split.

Can I change my split later?

Yes, as often as you like. New rules replace the old ones and apply from the next payment onward.

Do the people I pay need an account?

No. They just need a wallet address. Their share arrives there as cUSD automatically.

Can I stop using the ORYN agent?

Yes. Call setAgent from your wallet and ORYN loses every permission on your vault. Nothing else changes.
