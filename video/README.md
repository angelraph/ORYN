# How the demo video was made

[**Watch it on YouTube (3:20)**](https://youtu.be/lgaA1FE5e9o)

- **App footage is the live product.** Every app scene was recorded from oryn.click on Celo mainnet in Chrome at 1920×1080. The vault scenes use a read-only stand-in wallet that can report an address and nothing else, so nothing was signed or sent during recording.
- **The payment shown is real.** The receipt and proof scenes show the first independent payment into an ORYN vault and the agent's split that followed, both on Celo mainnet.
- **Motion graphics** (intro, the problem, AI agents, trust, outro) are HTML and CSS in the Celo brand palette, animated to the narration and captured frame by frame.
- **Narration** is Microsoft's Andrew neural voice. Captions are burned in, and timed per line from the voice track.
- **Assembly** with ffmpeg: H.264, 1080p, 30 fps.
